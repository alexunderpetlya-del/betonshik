v51.56 — Mobile controls rework

Implemented:
- Only ONE fixed joystick remains, positioned bottom-right.
- The right joystick controls movement (forward/back/strafe).
- Camera look is now swipe/drag directly on the gameplay canvas; no camera joystick.
- Multi-touch works as intended: one thumb can hold movement while the other swipes to look.
- Added radial deadzone and remapped response curve to the movement stick.
- Added safe-area-aware layout and larger touch targets.
- Context interaction/action buttons remain world-anchored and only appear when relevant.
- Center hotbar remains tappable.
- Added light haptic feedback where the browser/device supports navigator.vibrate().
- Added a short unobtrusive "swipe to look" hint that fades away automatically.
- Updated cache-bust query to v51.56.
