v51.59 — MOBILE PERFORMANCE + ADAPTIVE MENU LOADER

Mobile-only performance path:
- Desktop SCENE666 asset is byte-for-byte unchanged from v51.58. PC textures are not downscaled/recompressed.
- Mobile loads BETONSHCHIK_SCENE666_MOBILE.gltf.
- Mobile scene textures are repacked externally in quality tiers:
  * important large surfaces / vehicles / buildings: up to 1024
  * ordinary base-color environment textures: up to 768
  * normal / roughness / AO and vegetation: up to 512
  * small source textures remain at their native size
- Approx decoded scene texture footprint reduced from ~579 MB to ~209 MB on mobile.
- Geometry repacked into four Cloudflare-safe ~22 MB buffers.
- Grass and flower meshes are batched into InstancedMesh spatial chunks on mobile.
- Static imported mesh transforms are frozen after setup.
- Adaptive mobile DPR range: 0.78–1.35, with sustained-FPS hysteresis.
- Mobile sun shadow atlas stays 1024 and shadow refresh is throttled to ~20 Hz while moving / lower while idle.
- Hidden desktop 3D hotbar preview renders are skipped on mobile.
- Hidden minimap redraw is skipped.
- Desktop rendering/post FX and desktop SCENE666 remain unchanged.

Main menu:
- Added circular animated loading indicator in bottom-right.
- Responsive size/offset via clamp(), safe-area support, short landscape and large-screen rules.
- Spinner hides when the scene is ready and switches to error styling if loading fails.

Cloudflare:
