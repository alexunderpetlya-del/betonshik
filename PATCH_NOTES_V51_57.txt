Betonshik v51.57 — mobile control/UI rebuild

Changes:
- Context buttons no longer float next to world objects.
- One fixed left-thumb context button appears only while aiming at a valid object/NPC.
- When the hose is held, a separate fixed button appears: ВКЛ БЕТОН / ВЫКЛ БЕТОН.
- When the rake is equipped, the same tool-action button becomes РАВНЯТЬ and supports hold-to-rake.
- Right joystick remains the only joystick and controls movement.
- Camera look remains free swipe over gameplay canvas.
- Mobile look sensitivity increased ~26%.
- Joystick dead zone reduced from 11% to 5.5%, response curve made much quicker, sprint engages earlier.
- Desktop HUD is hidden completely on touch landscape.
- Added dedicated mobile HUD: money, stamina, fill/level, centered mobile hotbar, mobile toast, mobile aim dot.
- Mobile hotbar has its own touch targets and does not depend on hidden PC hotbar DOM.

Changed files:
- index.html
- main.js
- style.css
